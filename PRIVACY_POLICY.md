# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** February 9, 2026

## 1. Introduction

Thank you for using our Discord Bot ("the Bot", "Service"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights in relation to it.

By inviting the Bot to your Discord server or using its features, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

## 2. Information We Collect

To provide our enterprise-level services, including moderation, AI features, and utility commands, we may collect and store the following types of data:

### A. Automatically Collected Information (Discord API)

When the Bot interacts with the Discord API, we receive standard information including:

- **User Data:** User IDs, usernames, discriminators, and avatars.
- **Guild (Server) Data:** Guild IDs, names, channel lists, and role hierarchies.
- **Message Content:** The Bot processes message content to execute commands, perform AI-based moderation, and respond to queries. **We do not permanently store message content** unless explicitly required for a specific feature (e.g., logging/audit trails configured by server administrators).

### B. Data You Provide

- **Configuration Settings:** Custom prefixes, moderation rules, and toggleable settings for your specific server.
- **Support Queries:** Information provided when contacting our support team.

### C. Generated Data

- **Moderation Logs:** Records of warnings, mutes, kicks, and bans issued by the Bot, including reasons and timestamps.
- **Usage Statistics:** Anonymized data regarding command usage frequencies to help us improve performance.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data for the following purposes:

1.  **Service Provision:** To execute commands, manage server moderation, and provide AI-generated responses.
2.  **Improvement:** To analyze usage patterns and fix bugs or optimize features.
3.  **Compliance:** To enforce our Terms of Service and comply with Discord's Terms of Service.

## 4. Data Retention

We retain your personal information only for as long as is necessary for the purposes set out in this Privacy Policy.

- **Configuration Data:** Retained as long as the Bot is in your server.
- **Moderation Logs:** Retained to maintain a history of user infractions for server administrators.
- You may request the deletion of your data by contacting us (see Section 8).

## 5. Third-Party Sharing

We **do not** sell, trade, or rent your personal identification information to others.

We may share generic aggregated demographic information not linked to any personal identification information regarding visitors and users with our business partners and trusted affiliates.

**AI Providers:**
If you utilize the Bot's AI features (e.g., chatbots, AI moderation), text prompts may be sent to third-party Large Language Model (LLM) providers (such as OpenAI or Google) for processing. These providers are not permitted to use this data to train their models without anonymization, and we do not send personally identifiable metadata (like User IDs) to these providers unless necessary for the feature's context.

## 6. Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect the data we store. However, please remember that no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

## 7. Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

- **Right to Access:** Request copies of your personal data.
- **Right to Rectification:** Request correction of inaccurate information.
- **Right to Erasure:** Request that we delete your personal data ("Right to be Forgotten").

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the methods below.
